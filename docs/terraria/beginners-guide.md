## What Is Terraria, Really?

Terraria is a **2D action-adventure sandbox** where you dig, build, fight, and explore a procedurally generated world. On the surface it looks simple; underneath it's a deep action RPG with **four combat classes**, **30+ bosses**, and a progression arc that stretches from surface copper to space-faring Moon Lord fights.

**The core loop:** Chop and mine → build gear and shelter → explore new biomes → summon and beat bosses → unlock the next tier of ores and gear → go deeper. Kill the Wall of Flesh and the game **rebuilds itself into Hardmode** — a second, much harder act with new ores, new bosses, and new events.

**One thing to know before starting:** Terraria doesn't hold your hand. NPCs won't move in until you build them houses, bosses spawn when you *choose* to summon them (or stumble into their arena), and the first night is genuinely dangerous. That's the fun.

## Your First Night: Survive or Die

A full Terraria day is **15 real minutes** — about **9.4 minutes of daylight** (4:30 AM–7:30 PM), then nightfall brings zombies, flying eyes, and Demon Eyes. Your only goal on day one is to survive until **4:30 AM**. Here's the exact checklist.

![Terraria first-night survival checklist — chop, craft, mine, shelter, light, survive](/assets/images/terraria/terraria-first-night.webp)

| Step | What to do | Why it matters |
|:-----|:-----------|:---------------|
| 1️⃣ | **Chop 20–40 wood** with your axe | Wood is the foundation of every early recipe |
| 2️⃣ | Craft a **Workbench** (10 Wood, from your inventory menu) | Unlocks nearly every early crafting recipe |
| 3️⃣ | Craft a **Wooden Sword, Bow & Arrows** (or a Wooden Hammer first) | Your survival line — zombies hit hard at night |
| 4️⃣ | **Mine 20–40 stone** and dig some dirt | Stone becomes your furnace and mortar; dirt fills walls |
| 5️⃣ | **Build a shelter** — walls, background walls, a door | Background walls stop enemies spawning inside; a door keeps them out |
| 6️⃣ | Place a **torch** (wood + gel) and craft a **wooden bed** if you can | Light stops spawns near you; a bed sets your spawn point |
| 7️⃣ | **Stay inside from 7:30 PM** — fight from your doorway | Narrow doorways let you swing safely; never chase a zombie at night |

**Pro tip:** Punch the first few slimes — their **gel** combines with wood to make torches. Gather as much gel as you can early; you'll need it for the King Slime crown, torches, and the Slime Spear/Whip added in 1.4.5.

### Getting your first NPCs

NPCs don't wander in on their own — they need a **valid house**: an enclosed room with background walls, a **chair**, a **table**, and a **light source**, plus a door. Build one and wait for morning:

- **The Guide** arrives first, on day one. Talk to him — he gives crafting hints and material tips.
- **The Merchant** moves in once you hold **50+ silver coins** in your inventory. Save your first gold instead of spending it all on arrows.
- **The Demolitionist** arrives after you find an explosive, **The Nurse** once you boost your max health past 100, and so on — every NPC has a trigger.

## Pick a Class

Terraria doesn't lock you into a class, but specializing makes the game dramatically easier. Your class is decided by your **armor, weapon, and accessories** — and 1.4.5's new whips and summoner prefixes make the Summoner build more viable than ever.

| Class | Weapons | Armor line | Playstyle |
|:------|:--------|:-----------|:----------|
| ⚔️ **Melee** | Swords, yoyos, spears, boomerangs | Highest defense | Up close, high risk, high reward — the forgiving classic |
| 🏹 **Ranged** | Bows, guns, launchers | Medium defense | Keep your distance, always carry ammo, excellent single-target DPS |
| ✨ **Mage** | Wands, spell tomes, magic guns | Low defense, high mana | Massive area damage, but you're squishy and mana-hungry |
| 🐾 **Summoner** | Minions + whips | Lowest defense | Let your summons fight; whips boost their damage — the 1.4.5 favorite |

**My recommendation for a first playthrough:** go **Melee** until you've beaten the Eye of Cthulhu — the extra defense makes the learning curve much friendlier — then experiment. You can freely mix weapon types; the only real cost of "splashing" is that armor bonuses are class-specific.

> **New in 1.4.5:** whips got new Summoner prefixes and whip tags no longer stack, which rebalanced pure-Summoner. Summoners also got a second early-game whip path thanks to slime gel craftables — try the new **Slime Whip** in your first few nights.

## Tools & Armor: The Core Loop

Your entire early game is a climb through ore tiers. Each tier unlocks the next, so the rule is simple: **mine the best ore you can, craft the matching pickaxe and armor, then go deeper.** Here's the ladder.

| Ore tier | Where to find it | Craft before moving on |
|:---------|:-----------------|:-----------------------|
| **Copper / Tin** | Near the surface | First tools + armor; the very first tier |
| **Iron / Lead** | Upper underground | Iron pickaxe, anvils, buckets — the real workhorse tier |
| **Silver / Tungsten** | Mid underground | Better armor; a stepping stone to gold-tier gear |
| **Gold / Platinum** | Deep underground | Gold pickaxe + armor — the gate to the Desert and Jungle |
| **Demonite / Crimtane** | Corruption / Crimson | Found from first bosses; Demonite/Crimtane armor + pickaxe |
| **Hellstone** | The Underworld (bottom of the world) | **Molten armor** — the best pre-Hardmode set; requires the Nightmare/Deathbringer pickaxe to mine |
| **Cobalt / Palladium** | Hardmode, after breaking altars | The first Hardmode tier — beat the mechanical bosses with this |
| **Mythril / Orichalcum** | Hardmode, deeper | Second Hardmode tier; needs a Mythril Anvil |
| **Adamantite / Titanium** | Hardmode, very deep | Third Hardmode tier — endgame armor is built from here |

**Armor strategy:** Don't obsess over crafting every tier. Skip a tier when you can — e.g. go straight from Iron/Lead to Gold/Platinum — and spend the saved ore on weapons and accessories. One catch: **Molten gear is locked behind the Nightmare/Deathbringer pickaxe.** Gold-tier picks (55–59% pickaxe power) can't break Hellstone — beat Eater of Worlds / Brain of Cthulhu first, craft the Demonite/Crimtane pickaxe, and only then mine the Underworld. Defense is great, but **a bigger pickaxe and a better weapon win fights faster.**

## Biomes & Where to Go

The world opens up in a specific order. Here's the tour, roughly in difficulty order.

| Biome | Danger level | What you get |
|:------|:-------------|:-------------|
| **Forest** | Low | Your spawn area — wood, slimes, bunnies, early caves |
| **Desert** | Low–Medium | Cacti, antlions, and the Underground Desert with good early loot |
| **Snow / Tundra** | Medium | Ice-themed early gear, the Snowman mini-boss area, Ice biome chests |
| **Corruption / Crimson** | Medium–High | Your world's "evil" biome — Demonite/Crimtane, the first major boss |
| **Jungle** | High | The richest biome — rich loot, jungle armor, and Queen Bee (its signature ore, Chlorophyte, is Hardmode) |
| **Dungeon** | High (post-Skeletron) | A massive brick labyrinth with unique gear and the Old Man's boss |
| **Underworld** | Very High | Hellstone, fire imps, and the **Wall of Flesh** — the Hardmode gatekeeper |

**Digging strategy:** The underground has layers — dirt, then stone with ores, then the Cavern layer where the good stuff lives. Follow natural cave systems instead of tunneling straight down; they're faster and safer than a death shaft.

## Full Boss Order & How to Summon Each

Bosses are Terraria's progression milestones. Here's the full order, from your first slime to the Moon Lord, with the summon method for each.

![Terraria boss progression — pre-Hardmode, the Wall of Flesh, and the Hardmode endgame](/assets/images/terraria/terraria-boss-progression.webp)

### Pre-Hardmode

| Boss | How to summon | Reward / gate |
|:-----|:--------------|:--------------|
| **King Slime** | Craft a **Slime Crown** (Gel + Gold Crown) at a Demon/Crimson Altar, or find him randomly | Early cash and the Slimy Saddle |
| **Eye of Cthulhu** | Craft a **Suspicious Looking Eye** (6 Lens) at an altar; also spawns on its own after the first night with 200+ HP | Demonite/Crimtane ore, first real gear jump |
| **Eater of Worlds** (Corruption) | **Worm Food** (Vile Powder + Rotten Chunk) at an altar, or smash 3 Shadow Orbs | Shadow Scale — Demonite armor & pickaxe |
| **Brain of Cthulhu** (Crimson) | **Bloody Spine** (Vicious Powder + Vertebrae) at an altar, or smash 3 Crimson Hearts | Tissue Sample — Crimtane armor & pickaxe |
| **Queen Bee** | **Abeemination** (Honey Block + Stinger + Bottled Honey) in the Jungle, or break a Larva | Bee gear, the best pre-Hardmode ranged weapon |
| **Skeletron** | Talk to the **Old Man** at the Dungeon door at night | Unlocks the Dungeon — the best pre-Hardmode loot |
| **Wall of Flesh** | Throw a **Guide Voodoo Doll** into lava in the Underworld | **Triggers Hardmode** — the game's big reset |

### Hardmode

Defeating the Wall of Flesh corrupts your world, spawns the **Hallow**, and unlocks three new ore tiers. The boss ladder continues:

| Boss | How to summon | Notes |
|:-----|:--------------|:------|
| **The Twins** | **Mechanical Eye** (Iron/Lead Bar + Lens + Soul of Light) | Eye of Cthulhu's angry, screen-filling upgrade |
| **The Destroyer** | **Mechanical Worm** (Iron/Lead Bar + Rotten Chunk/Vertebrae + Soul of Night) | A giant worm; the easiest of the three with AoE weapons |
| **Skeletron Prime** | **Mechanical Skull** (Iron/Lead Bar + Bone + Soul of Night) | Skeletron, but with a chainsaw arm |
| **Plantera** | Break a **Plantera's Bulb** in the Underground Jungle | Appears only after all three mechanical bosses fall |
| **Golem** | Use a **Lihzahrd Power Cell** on the altar in the Jungle Temple | Plantera's key drops unlock the Temple entrance |
| **Lunatic Cultist** | Defeat the **four cultists** at the Dungeon entrance | Unlocks the **Lunar Events** |
| **Moon Lord** | Survive the **four Celestial Pillars**, then it descends | **The final boss** — beat it and you've "completed" Terraria |

> **Boss-order rule of thumb:** you should be able to beat each boss comfortably with the gear from the tier before it. If a boss wrecks you, you're under-geared — upgrade armor and accessories first, and build a proper arena with platforms, campfires, and heart lanterns.

## Hardmode: The Game Opens Up

Hardmode isn't just "harder monsters" — it's a **content expansion**:

- **New ores** spawn when you break Demon/Crimson Altars (Cobalt → Mythril → Adamantite), letting you craft increasingly powerful armor.
- **The Corruption/Crimson and the new Hallow** spread across your world. The Hallow is mostly friendly; the evil biomes will eventually threaten your builds unless you contain them.
- **New events** appear: the Pirate Invasion, Solar Eclipse, and the post-Plantera Martian Madness — plus the Pumpkin and Frost Moons.
- **New NPCs** arrive, including the Steampunker (whose Clentaminator is the best tool for stopping biome spread) and the Cyborg.

Most new-world content from 1.4.5 only appears in **worlds created after the update**, so if you're coming back to Terraria for the update, start fresh — old saves won't see the new items.

## Pro Tips

- **Always carry a rope and a platform stack.** Climbing out of a deep cave is the #1 beginner death.
- **Keep a spare wood stockpile.** Wood is needed for torches, furniture, and NPC houses constantly.
- **Make an arena for every boss.** A flat platform strip with campfires, a heart lantern, and honey pools turns a nightmare fight into a win.
- **Use the new 1.4.5 crafting-from-chests** — load up a storage room near your workbench and stop juggling materials in your inventory.
- **Fishing is worth it.** Fishing quests give useful rewards and the crates contain ores and items that skip grinding.
- **The Guide is your wiki.** Ask him "Crafting" with any material and he tells you what it makes — he's genuinely the best in-game reference.
- **Don't sell gel, lens, or soul fragments.** All three are boss-summon ingredients you'll want by the bucket.
- **Defense beats damage early.** Until you're comfortable dodging, pick the armor with more defense every time.

## FAQ

**Q: How long is the Terraria day?**
A full day/night cycle is 15 real minutes — about **9.4 minutes of daylight** (4:30 AM–7:30 PM) and **5.6 minutes of night** (7:30 PM–4:30 AM).

**Q: What's the best class for a beginner?**
Melee. The extra defense keeps you alive while you learn boss patterns. Ranged is the next-easiest, and Summoner is the most powerful late-game if you know what you're doing.

**Q: Do I need to play Terraria 1.4.5 in a new world?**
Yes — most of the new 1.4.5 items only spawn in worlds created after the update. Start fresh.

**Q: How do I stop the Corruption/Crimson from spreading?**
The Hallow counters the evil biomes, and the Steampunker's **Clentaminator** (Hardmode) is the reliable fix. Dig 3-block-wide gaps around your base early to keep the spread out.

**Q: What's the actual final boss?**
The **Moon Lord**, summoned after you survive the four Celestial Pillars in the endgame. Beating it unlocks the endgame celebration — but events, fishing, and building keep the game alive long after.

**Q: Is crossplay out?**
It's rolling out in phases through 2026. PC, Mobile, and Dedicated Servers are already cross-compatible as of spring 2026; PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch integration is being certified next.

**Q: Terraria or Core Keeper / Necesse?**
Terraria is the densest of the three and the one with the deepest boss progression. [Core Keeper](/core-keeper/) is a gentler co-op take on the same formula, and [Necesse](/necesse/) trades Terraria's handcrafted feel for colony automation.
