## What is Terraria?

Terraria is a **2D action-adventure sandbox** developed and published by **Re-Logic**. You spawn into a procedurally generated world with nothing but a copper shortsword and an axe, and from there the game hands you total freedom: dig for ores, craft gear, build houses, summon bosses, and push deeper through biomes until you're facing the Moon Lord in space. It's often described as "Minecraft in 2D," but that undersells it — Terraria's combat, class system, and boss design make it closer to a 2D action RPG wearing a sandbox's clothes.

## Game Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| 🏰 **Building & Housing** | Build NPC houses, arenas, and full bases out of thousands of block types |
| ⚔️ **Four Classes** | Melee, Ranged, Mage, and Summoner — each with its own gear, weapons, and playstyle |
| 👹 **30+ Bosses** | From the Eye of Cthulhu to the Moon Lord, plus event bosses and mini-bosses |
| ⛏️ **Deep Progression** | Copper → Hellstone → Hardmode ores → endgame armors across dozens of tiers |
| 🌍 **Biomes & Secrets** | Corruption/Crimson, Jungle, Dungeon, Underworld, the Hallow, and secret world seeds |
| 🎣 **Fishing & Events** | Fishing quests, Blood Moons, Solar Eclipses, and four seasonal/holiday events |
