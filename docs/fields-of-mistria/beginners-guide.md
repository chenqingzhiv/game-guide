## What Is Fields of Mistria?

Fields of Mistria is a single-player, cozy farming life-sim with a retro anime art style — the developer's stated inspiration is 1990s anime like *Sailor Moon*. You arrive in the coastal town of **Mistria** after an earthquake has cracked the land and woken old magic. The town is rebuilding, your farm is overgrown, and the valley's dragon guardian, **Caldarus**, has been reduced to a broken statue on your land. Repair it, feed it **Essence**, and you start unlocking the game's deeper systems — skill perks, magic spells, and eventually the dungeon under the town.

## Before You Start: Know the Two Currencies

Fields of Mistria has two currencies and mixing them up is the most common early mistake.

- **Tesserae (T)** — the money. Seeds, tools, furniture, and town purchases all take T.
- **Essence** — the purple-orbit currency that falls from farming, mining, fishing, and clearing debris. You spend it at **Caldarus statues** to buy permanent skill perks, and later to craft **Essence Stones** that power the automated Sprite Statues.

The rule: **don't spend Essence on anything that costs T**, and don't burn your first Essence on flashy perks — the early skill tree has a few that pay for themselves fast (see the Mines section below).

## Your First Spring: The Daily Routine

Seasons in Fields of Mistria run **28 days**, and that number drives everything. Unwatered crops skip a day of growth, and **any crop still in the ground when the season turns dies** — no partial credit. So the day-to-day loop is:

1. **Water everything first.** This is non-negotiable. Crops die at season change, and missed waterings snowball into a harvest that never arrives.
2. **Fishing and foraging fill the gaps.** On the beach and along the Eastern Road you'll find shells, glowing items, and fish. Fish sell for a healthy early income — common ones run 30–45 T, and a Goldfish goes for 120 T.
3. **Hit the Request Board.** Villager requests pay money, materials, and **Renown** — the town reputation that unlocks the Mines. It's the closest thing to free progress.
4. **Watch the calendar.** Stop planting 2–3 days before the season ends, or you're planting seeds you'll never harvest.
5. **Sleep before you collapse.** If your HP hits zero in the Mines you faint and the day ends immediately, losing your progress. In town, just get to bed — the next day starts fresh.

**Stamina management is the real gameplay.** Your energy bar is the main limiter in the first Spring, so treat these as free refills:

- **Sleeping Dragon Inn** — one free bowl of soup per day (restores 20 HP and energy).
- **Bathhouse** and the fountains (one on the Eastern Road, one near the beach fish shop) restore stamina.
- **Stamina Up** items — permanent energy upgrades earned through Town Rank — are your best long-term spend.

## The Best Spring Crops & Early Money

Your starter seeds are **turnips**, and they're the safe first move: **25 T per seed, 40 T at sale, 4 days to grow.** Plant a modest patch — don't overplant what you can't water in one pass.

Once you have a cash buffer around 500–800 T, the Spring meta opens up:

| Crop | Seed cost | Sells for | Time to harvest | Notes |
|:-----|:----------|:----------|:----------------|:------|
| 🌱 **Turnip** | 25 T | 40 T | 4 days | The safe starter; cheap, fast, low risk |
| 🥕 **Carrot** | 40 T | 80 T | 6 days | Solid mid-tier profit per day |
| 🥔 **Potato** | 40 T | 75 T | 6 days | Bake it for 80 T — cooking beats raw selling |
| 🥬 **Cabbage** | 70 T | 180 T | 9 days | Best one-time payout once you have savings |
| 🌸 **Strawberry** | 300 T | 125 T | 9 days, then regrows every 4 | The season-long earner — buy once, harvest 4–5 times |
| 🫛 **Pea** | 300 T | 135 T | 5 days, then regrows every 3 | Even better gold-per-day than strawberries |

The biggest upgrade in the game isn't a crop — it's **regrowing crops**. Strawberries and peas cost real money up front, but they keep producing all season, which is what turns a farm into a money printer. When the seasons roll over: Tea is the Summer pick, Onions in Fall, Snow Peas in Winter.

**The three-stream early strategy** (this is what the Bilibili guides converge on):

1. **Fish first** — before you can afford good seeds, fish to build capital, then reinvest in seeds.
2. **Plant cheap fast crops** (turnips) for a steady cash loop; reinvest most of the income into more seeds.
3. **Add quests and museum donations** — they pay Renown, which unlocks the Mines and permanent stamina upgrades.

**Cooking and mining beat raw selling.** A 75 T potato bakes into an 80 T Baked Potato. Copper tools sell for 480 T each, and a copper ingot goes for 110 T. Once the Mines open, selling tools is a legitimate mid-game income.

## Tools & the Upgrade Order

You start with a **Worn Hoe** and **Worn Watering Can** plus a few spring seeds. The upgrade order the community settles on:

1. **Watering Can** — water more crops per day, which is your biggest time sink.
2. **Pickaxe** — faster progress in the Mines, where the real ores live.
3. **Axe** — wood for every building project.
4. **Hoe / Shovel** — field prep and digging.
5. **Fishing rod and bug net** — nice to have; not the priority in Spring.

Three mechanical facts worth knowing early:

- **You can craft your own tools.** Four ingots at the Blacksmith's anvil make a tool — cheaper than the 500 T the shop charges once you have the smelting loop going.
- **Charge your strikes.** Hold the tool button to charge; upgraded metal tiers widen the area. A **Silver Pickaxe** (4 Silver Ingots, Blacksmithing level 4) breaks a **3×6 area** per charged swing.
- **Smelting is 10 ore → 1 ingot**, but a Blacksmithing perk drops that to **9 ore per ingot** — one of the first perks worth buying.

## The Mines: Ore Floors, Monsters & the 99-Floor Dungeon

The Mines unlock through the **"Reopening the Mines" quest** once your town reaches **Stone Town Rank** (earned via requests and museum donations). Before heading in, do the **"Do Bro a Favor"** quest — it hands you a free Pickaxe.

The Mines are checkpoint-based: push down to a new **elevator checkpoint every 5 floors**, then dive again with better gear. Ore is tied to specific floors:

| Zone | Floors | Ore & loot |
|:-----|:-------|:-----------|
| ⛏️ **Upper Mines** | 1–19 | Copper Ore, Ruby |
| 🌀 **Water Seal** | 20 | Gate to the Tide Caverns — unlocks the **Summon Rain** spell |
| 🌊 **Tide Caverns** | 21–39 | Iron Ore, Sapphire, Coral, Tidestone artifacts |
| ⛰️ **Earth Seal** | 40 | Gate to the Deep Earth — unlocks the **Growth** spell |
| 🏔️ **Deep Earth** | 41–59 | Silver Ore, Emerald (needs Iron Pickaxe or better) |
| 🌋 **Fire Seal** | 60 | Gate to the Lava Caves — unlocks **Dragon's Breath** |
| 🔥 **Lava Caves** | 61–79 | Gold Ore |
| 🌑 **Void Seal** | 80 | Gate to the Ancient Ruins — the "Breaking the Ruins Seal" quest |
| 🏛️ **Ancient Ruins** | 81–99 | Mistril Ore |

Every biome jump is a gear check. **Silver** is the big mid-game milestone: the Silver Pickaxe's charged strike breaks a 3×6 area, and the efficient way to farm Silver Ore is to elevator-jump between floors 45, 50, and 55, break the ore, then reset the mine layout.

**Combat is real.** Monsters drop crafting materials and raise your Combat skill. If your HP hits zero you faint and the day ends — pack a heal. Enemy patterns worth learning:

- **Glob enemies** stun themselves when they land — hit them then.
- **Enchanterns** (lanterns) hop 7–8 times before losing charge and fleeing.
- **Rock spitters** take little sword damage, but heavy damage when you hit their projectiles back at them.
- **Mimics** (shaking chests) can be fought, but you get better rewards by **feeding them**.

Spend your early Essence at the **broken Caldarus statue in the Mines** on the perk that makes ingots cost 9 ore instead of 10 — it compounds on every ingot you'll ever smelt.

## Magic, Essence & the Dragon Caldarus

Magic is Fields of Mistria's signature twist, and it unlocks through a tidy progression:

- **Essence** falls from daily activities — farm debris, mining, fishing. Purple orbs don't despawn if you miss them, so you can come back.
- **The farm statue** (Caldarus' statue on your property, behind a tree that Eiland helps cut down) sells perks for **Archaeology, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Farming, Fishing, Ranching, and Woodcrafting**.
- **The broken Mines statue** sells **Combat and Mining** perks.

**To unlock magic itself:** reach **Level 10 in the Mines**, then return to the farm statue — Caldarus grants you the **Full Restore** spell and your first **Mana Point**. Cast with the C key, and you can pin one spell at a time from the Magic Spells menu.

| Spell | Unlock | Mana | Effect |
|:------|:-------|:-----|:-------|
| 💚 **Full Restore** | Mines Level 10 | 1 | Fully restores Health and Stamina |
| 🌧️ **Summon Rain** | Water Seal (floor 20) | 1 | Rains — waters your crops for you |
| 🌱 **Growth** | Earth Seal (floor 40) | 2 | Grows crops in a 3×3 area; advances trees by one stage |
| 🔥 **Dragon's Breath** | Fire Seal (floor 60) | 2 | Offensive fire spell; also breaks rock barriers |

**Mana** comes back slowly — sleeping restores about a quarter point per day, and rare loot items restore it instantly. The **Fire Seal** is the milestone that opens the endgame: break it (the Sealing Scroll costs **10 Silver Ingots, 10 Sapphires, 10 Rubies, 10 Emeralds**), and Caldarus appears in human form, teaches you Dragon's Breath, and retreats to his temple in the **Deep Woods** — which you unlock by burning the vine barrier above the Carpenter's Shop.

**Sprite Statues** are the automation layer, unlocked after Dragon's Breath and powered by **Essence Stones** (crafted at the Stone Refinery, unlocked at Town Rank 55):

- **Water Sprite Statue** — in a cave east of the Beach near the lighthouse; waters a **5×5** area of crops daily.
- **Animal Sprite Statue** — in a cave near The Narrows; pets your animals for you.

Essence Stone sizes range from Tiny (5 Stone, lasts 1 day) to Large (40 Stone + 100 Essence, lasts 20 days).

## Ranching & Animals

Animals arrive after you build a barn or coop. The basics:

- **Pet and feed daily** — that's what drives your **Ranching** skill.
- **Let animals out in good weather** — it also counts toward Ranching.
- Later perks add **auto-feeders** and the **Big Bell** (calls all animals in or out).

Ranching is a slower payoff than crops, but the animal products sell well and the 1.0 perks make the chore fade away — **Eggstra** and **Close Bond II** in the Ranching tree are worth saving Essence for.

## The Town, Museum & Renown

Mistria runs on **Renown** — town reputation earned through requests, quests, and museum donations. It's your real progression bar:

- **Renown caps at 100** in 1.0, with the new **Mistril Town Rank** event at the top.
- **The Museum** accepts donations (fish, artifacts, bugs, crops) and is fully completable in 1.0 — two new sets (the **Honey Set** and **Terrarium Treasures Set**) finish it out.
- **The Town Repair questline is now completable** — the earthquake aftermath finally gets fixed.
- **Saturday Market** gains two new vendors in 1.0: **Stillwell** (adds a Mission Board with repeatable missions) and **Zorel** (sells Crystal Resonators and Song Crystals to change the music on your farm and in your home).
- **House exterior restyling** unlocks after you fully upgrade the house.
- **Friday Night at the Inn** — a new storyline that opens after you finish the Mines storyline.

Town Rank also hands out **Stamina Up** items, which are the game's best permanent upgrade.

## Romance & Marriage in 1.0

The 1.0 headline is the completed relationship system. Fields of Mistria has **12 marriage candidates**, and you can romance any of them regardless of your character's gender:

**Adeline, Balor, Caldarus, Celine, Eiland, Hayden, Juniper, March, Reina, Ryis, Seridia, and Valen.**

Two of them were hidden during Early Access and fully unlocked in 1.0: **Caldarus** (the dragon) and **Seridia**.

The path to the altar:

1. **Gift and chat daily.** Everyone has liked and loved items — giving loved items matters far more than liked ones. (Juniper loves rare artifacts and fine food and hates bugs, if you want a concrete example.)
2. **Dating.** Reach a certain heart threshold and you can choose the romantic route with that character.
3. **10 hearts.** The heart cap rose from 8 to **10** in 1.0. Hit 10 with someone you're dating and a **10-Heart Proposal Event** unlocks.
4. **The ring.** Elsie mails you the **Engagement Ring** recipe the morning after you hit 10 hearts — craft it at the Forge, then propose.
5. **Wedding customization.** Before the ceremony you plan flower color, cake flavor, and reception meal with your fiancé.
6. **Family.** A few weeks after the wedding, a **Mystical Feather** cutscene opens the optional family-planning path — children with their own events.

**The dating-multiple-NPCs wrinkle:** getting engaged automatically drops your other relationships from Dating to Best Friends. Two new 1.0 settings let you hide the break-up letters or disable the status change entirely — but you have to toggle them before confirming the engagement.

At $13.99 it's the same price as Stardew, with an art style nothing else in the genre matches.

## Pro Tips & Common Mistakes

### ✅ Do This

- **Water crops before anything else, every day.** Unwatered crops skip growth; crops still planted at the season change die. This is the whole game in Spring.
- **Fishing is your day-one income.** Common fish sell for 30–45 T, a Goldfish for 120 T. Fish the three ponds on the Eastern Road, then exit and re-enter to respawn them.
- **Take the free soup at the inn daily** (20 HP + energy), and use the bathhouse and fountains.
- **Buy the 10-slot backpack upgrade** at the general store for 1,000 T early — inventory space is a tax on everything.
- **Get to Stone Town Rank fast** (requests + museum donations) — it unlocks the Mines.
- **Buy the ingot-cost perk at the Mines statue** (9 ore instead of 10) — it pays off on every ingot.
- **Craft your own tools** once you can smelt — four ingots beats paying 500 T.
- **Switch to regrowing crops** (Strawberries/Peas in Spring) as soon as you have ~500–800 T saved.

### ❌ Do Not Do This

- **Don't plant past day ~25 of a season** — the crops will rot at the season change with no credit.
- **Don't overplant what you can't water.** Stamina is the real limit in Spring; a huge field you can't water is a sunk cost.
- **Don't spend Essence on T-priced things or flashy perks early.** The early tree has perks that pay for themselves; pick those.
- **Don't dive into the Mines without a heal and stamina food** — fainting ends the day and wastes your progress.
- **Don't skip the museum.** Donations are the fastest Renown, and Renown is your real progression gate.
- **Don't expect a deep dungeon-crawler.** The Mines are a cozy game's dungeon — fun, but not a full ARPG.
- **Don't expect cross-platform cloud saves or a Mac build.** Windows and Linux only.

## FAQ

### What is Fields of Mistria?
A cozy farming life-sim from **NPC Studio** set in a coastal town rebuilding after an earthquake. You farm, ranch, fish, mine a 99-floor dungeon, learn magic from a dragon, and romance 12 candidates. It left Early Access on **August 5, 2026** at an Overwhelmingly Positive rating.

### Is Fields of Mistria worth it in 2026?
At **$13.99** (less during the launch week), yes. It's the same price as Stardew Valley with a distinctive 90s-anime art style, a real dungeon and combat system, and a finished marriage system. If you want the deepest long-haul farm sim, Stardew still wins; if you want something that fights back and looks like *Sailor Moon*, this is it.

### How long is Fields of Mistria?
The town repair story, dungeon, and romance arcs run **30–60+ hours** depending on how much you grind. Seasons are **28 days**, and there's no time limit — the clock pressure is all daily stamina.

### What's the best way to make money early?
Fish first, then plant turnips for a steady cash loop, and reinvest into regrowing crops (Strawberries/Peas in Spring). Add Request Board jobs and museum donations for Renown. Once the Mines open, cooking and selling tools are solid mid-game income.

### How do I unlock magic in Fields of Mistria?
Reach **Level 10 in the Mines**, then return to Caldarus' statue on your farm — you get the **Full Restore** spell and your first Mana Point. More spells unlock by breaking the seal floors at Mines 20, 40, and 60.

### Can you get married in Fields of Mistria?
Yes — since 1.0. All **12 candidates** are marriageable regardless of your character's gender. Reach **10 hearts**, trigger the proposal event, craft the Engagement Ring (recipe from Elsie), plan a customizable wedding, and a few weeks later a **Mystical Feather** cutscene opens the optional children path.

### Is there combat in Fields of Mistria?
Yes. The Mines are a real dungeon with monsters, ores, and seal-floor bosses. Monsters drop crafting materials and raise your Combat skill, and fainting costs you the day.

### Is Fields of Mistria on Steam Deck?
Yes — it's **Steam Deck Verified**, with a native Linux build and full controller support. No Mac support, no console version, and cloud saves are not cross-platform.
