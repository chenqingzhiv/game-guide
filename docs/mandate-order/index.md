# Mandate Order 🏯

**Build a Warring-State city where farmers become soldiers.** A gridless city-builder fused with real-time tactics — grow a 30-person village into a walled kingdom in ancient China.

## What is Mandate Order?

Mandate Order (烽沙) is an **Eastern war-themed city-builder** from Chengdu indie team **Digital Sky**, set in China's Spring and Autumn / Warring States period. You start as a frontier city-keeper with one village of about thirty workers and build it into a walled, diplomatically active state — farming on a real four-season calendar, governing through the **six schools of thought** (Confucian, Daoist, Mohist, Legalist, Military, Agricultural), and fighting real-time tactical battles with soldiers conscripted from your own workforce. It launched into **Steam Early Access on August 12, 2026** at **$19.99 (¥68)** with a roughly 12-month road to 1.0.

## Game Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| 🏘️ **伍 Worker System** | Workers arrive in squads of five, each needing food, water, housing and winter fuel |
| 🌾 **Four-Season Farming** | Real agrarian calendar — spring planting, summer weeding, autumn harvest, winter storage |
| 🏮 **Six Schools of Thought** | Recruit retainers from Confucian, Daoist, Mohist, Legalist, Military and Agricultural schools |
| 🏛️ **Rank & Diplomacy** | Earn the 爵位 title and found your state to unlock diplomacy with the Seven Warring States |
| 🏯 **Living Walls** | Free-form walls, gates and towers that genuinely decide siege outcomes |
| ⚔️ **Real-Time Tactics** | Conscript farmers into infantry, archers and chariots; line-drawing formation commands; thousand-soldier battles |
| 🛠️ **Siege Engines** | Crossbow turrets (床弩) and catapults (投石车), boosted by the Mohist and Military schools |
