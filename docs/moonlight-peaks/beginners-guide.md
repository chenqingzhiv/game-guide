# Moonlight Peaks: Beginner's Guide - First 10 Nights

## Your Vampire Schedule

Wake at 6 PM, sleep by 6 AM. Roughly 15 real minutes per night.

## Night 1: Tutorial
- Follow your uncle, get starter tools
- Till 3x3 plot, plant Wild Potatoes
- Explore toward Moonlit Pines

## Nights 2-3: Real Start
- Visit Luna's seed stall (buy Wild Potato, Onion, White Grape)
- Expand to 6x6 field
- Meet NPCs: Luna, Ridge (blacksmith), Sabrina, Orlock

## Nights 4-6: Quests
- "The Need for Herbs" unlocks tools, fishing, recipes
- Start Orlock's Wine Scheme for Keg blueprint
- Start Copper tool upgrades (1,000g + 3 Copper Bars each)

## Nights 7-8: Magic
- Repair wand via "The Magic of Crops"
- Learn Aquaflux I (auto-water with Mana)
- Unlock Hellkitten Form (faster, less stamina)

## Nights 9-10: Building
- Build 3 Kegs (20 Wood each)
- Build Barn (4,000g) for animals and fertilizer
- Start magic crop rotation

## 10 Things I Wish I Knew
1. Schedule is 6 PM to 6 AM
2. Don't overplant (6x6 is enough)
3. Use Hellkitten form
4. Process everything (Kegs = real money)
5. Save for tool upgrades
6. Mana and Stamina are separate
7. Check map for sparkle icons
8. Watch the seasonal calendar
9. Unlock fishing early
10. Collect 100 Soul Blobs for Antique Clock
