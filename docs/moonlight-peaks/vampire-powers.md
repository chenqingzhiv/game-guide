# Moonlight Peaks: Vampire Powers & Spells Guide

## Wand Repair
Complete "The Magic of Crops" quest (early Summer).

## Spells
| Spell | Effect | Priority |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Aquaflux I | Auto-water crops with Mana | Critical |
| Ethereal Axe | Auto-chop trees | High |
| Ethereal Pickaxe | Auto-mine ores | High |
| Hellkitten Form | Faster movement, less stamina | Critical |

## Stamina vs Mana
Stamina: farming/chopping/mining. Recover via food.
Mana: spells. Recover via Mana Extractor, Moonfruit Jam, Nightshade.

## Potions
Alter Ego Elixir (change appearance)
Fluent Fishing Tonic (easier fishing)
Mindless Miner Tonic (better mining)
Love Potion (boost romance)

## Extend Night
100 Soul Blobs for Death = Antique Clock (25 min nights).
