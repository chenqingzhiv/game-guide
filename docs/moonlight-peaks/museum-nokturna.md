# Moonlight Peaks: Museum & Nokturna

## Museum (Fall Y1)
Unlock: Talk to Jada, gather artifacts, clear park, invitations.

Donate in each collection room. Key reward at 10%: Firefly Pollinator.

Collections: Crops (2-star), Fish, Bugs, Forage, Minerals, Artifacts.

## Nokturna Card Game
Gwent-style CCG. 20-card deck, 2 copies max, 60-point cap.

Types: Deity, Aquatic, Plant, Vampire, Werewolf, Location, Item, Spell, Animal.

Collect: Defeat NPCs, explore, dig, fish, shops.

Tips: Prioritize card draw, build around a type, watch the 60-point cap.
