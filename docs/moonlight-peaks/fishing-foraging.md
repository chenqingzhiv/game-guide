# Moonlight Peaks: Fishing, Foraging & Bug Catching

## Fishing
Unlock: Find Noel, catch 3 fish.

Rod upgrades: Basic (free) -> Premium (3,500-16,000g) -> Enchanted (48,000g, zero stamina).

Tips: Look for ripples, use Fluent Fishing Tonic, save fish for Gobbler.

## Foraging
Mushrooms (Nightshade restores Mana), Flowers (gifts), Dig spots (coins).
Foragables respawn each night.

## Bug Catching
Unlock: Orlock chain to Death (~Night 12).
Soul Blobs: Collect 100 for Antique Clock (25 min nights).

## Mining
Cave of Echoes (Misty Shores, southwest of town): Copper anywhere, Iron in the deeper grey/silver rocks, Gold in the eastern lake and northern ledge areas (needs Iron Pickaxe + Mermaid/Bat form).

Full ore locations, smelting recipes and pickaxe tiers: [Mining & Cave of Echoes](mining-caves.md).
