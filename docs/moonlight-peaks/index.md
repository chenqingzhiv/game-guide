## First Steps

1. Meet your uncle for the tutorial
2. Clear a small 3x3 field - don't overplant
3. Plant Wild Potatoes (cheap, fast, 3 per harvest)
4. Explore Moonlit Pines and meet Luna
5. Complete "The Need for Herbs" quest
