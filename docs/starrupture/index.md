## What is StarRupture?

StarRupture is a **first-person open-world survival + factory automation** game from **Creepy Jar** — the Polish studio behind *Green Hell*. You're a prisoner serving a sentence on **Arcadia-7**, mining and manufacturing for the five corporations that run the planet, while its sun **Ruptura** periodically erupts and scours the surface. Between eruptions you build conveyor-fed production lines: excavators mine ore, rails carry it to smelters and fabricators, and the **Orbital Cargo Launcher** ships finished goods to orbit for reputation and tech unlocks. It launched into Steam Early Access on **January 6, 2026**, and quickly climbed to the top of Steam's best-seller charts.

## Game Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| ⛏️ **Factory Automation** | Excavators, rails, smelters, fabricators — a full conveyor-fed production chain |
| 🔥 **Rupture Cycle** | A periodic solar cataclysm that resets the surface and spawns rare materials |
| 🏭 **Five Corporations** | Moon Energy, Selenian, Clever Robotics, Griffits Blue & Future Health gate every unlock (a tutorial-only Training track covers the first hour) |
| 🛠️ **Research & Blueprints** | Analyzing Station → Data Points → Recipe Station; advanced recipes from Abandoned Settlement Blue Chests |
| 👾 **Vermin Combat** | Alien bug hordes, from Flingers and Exploders to the armored Goliath boss |
| 🏗️ **Base Building** | Platform-first construction with power grids, rail networks, and automated defense |
| 🌍 **Procedural World** | Seeded alien landscapes with volcanic zones, crystal caves, and pure ore nodes |
