# 🧊 Valheim Deep North Complete Guide — Survival, Gear & the Frost King

*Last updated: August 2026 | Game version: Deep North update*

---

The Deep North is Valheim's **eighth and final biome** — a frozen wasteland at the top of the world where the sun never fully rises and the aurora never stops dancing. The Deep North update adds the whole frozen region, a complete **cold-survival rework**, two new ships built for ice, a new endgame crafting tier at the **Frost Forge**, and a genuinely brutal final boss: the **Frost King**.

This guide covers everything you need to know — getting there, staying warm, gearing up, and taking the Frost King down for good. Still early in your run? The [Beginner's Guide](/valheim/beginners-guide/) walks the whole journey up to this point.

## What Is the Deep North?

The Deep North occupies the polar regions of Valheim's procedurally generated world — the northernmost point beyond the Ashlands. It's the final frontier of the game, and it's not a place you can just wander into.

**What makes it different from every other biome:**

- **Constant twilight** — the sun never fully rises. Ambient light is a dim blue-gray that makes the whole region feel like a dream.
- **Aurora Borealis** — dynamic aurora effects shift through green, blue, and purple, casting moving light across the ice. It's the prettiest biome in the game — right up until a blizzard hits.
- **Blizzard events** — random weather that cuts visibility to ~10 meters, drains cold resistance faster, and extinguishes any campfire not protected by walls.
- **Ice caves** — procedural cave systems beneath the ice cap, with warm interior pockets and the biome's most important rare resources.

> 🧊 **Rule of thumb:** if you can beat the Ashlands comfortably, you're *ready to start* Deep North. If the Ashlands still gives you trouble, you'll have a very bad time here.

## How to Get to the Deep North

The Deep North sits at the very top of your world map. There's no portal network up there — you have to sail.

### Ships That Can Handle the Ice

| Ship | Best For | Requirements |
|:-----|:---------|:-------------|
| **Snekkja** | Scouting the coast, threading ice channels, reaching the shore first | 30 Iron Nails, 30 Hearthwood, 10 Deer Hide |
| **Icebreaker** | Your main transport — breaks through thin ice, fastest in open water | 50 Iron Nails, 20 Fine Wood, 10 Hearthwood, 5 Deer Hide |

The **Icebreaker** is the workhorse. Its metal-reinforced bow smashes through thin ice sheets instead of getting stuck, and it's faster than the longship in open water. Equip it with the **Ram** upgrade and you can even damage enemies on contact while sailing.

The **Snekkja** is a fast scout boat with a shallow draft — it can slip through water that would beach a karve or longship. Equip the **Depth Finder** upgrade to get a warning before you run aground in shallow, ice-choked waters.

If you somehow arrive without an Icebreaker, a **Moder-powered sail** still gets you across the open ocean — just don't expect to push through ice sheets. Park at the coast and walk.

### Before You Sail

1. **Build a forward base** on the nearest accessible shore *outside* the Deep North, with a portal back to your main base.
2. **Stock it** with the Ashlands-tier food, meads, and gear you'd take into a long expedition.
3. **Bring the new materials.** Hearthwood for emergency repairs, Arctic Moss for the Heartystew recipe, and enough iron nails to build a shelter.
4. **Set your spawn point** at the forward base. You *will* die here at least once.

## The Deep North Biome — Terrain, Hazards & Resources

### Terrain & Dangers

| Hazard | What It Does | How to Handle |
|:-------|:-------------|:--------------|
| Blizzard events | 10m visibility, drains cold resist faster, kills unprotected campfires | Shelter in caves or a walled base; don't travel during storms |
| Freezing water | Drops core temperature fast — swimming is a death sentence | Stick to the Icebreaker; never swim between ice floes |
| Frost Golems | Tough melee mobs — the main source of Frost Cores | Parry their telegraphed swings; fire arrows deal bonus damage |
| Frost Wisps (wild) | Small adds that explode on proximity | Kite them or kill at range before they reach you |
| Thin ice | Collapses underfoot over deep water | Watch for darker ice; Snekkja and Icebreaker handle it safely |

### Resources

| Resource | Where to Find | Used For |
|:---------|:--------------|:---------|
| **Permafrost Stone** | Mining ice walls | Deep North building base |
| **Frost Core** | Frost Golem drops | Frost resistance gear, Frost Forge |
| **Glacial Crystal** | Ice cave mining | Tier-3 upgrade materials |
| **Arctic Moss** | Harvested from cave walls | Healing meads, frost-resistant potions, Heartystew |
| **Hearthwood** | Harvesting frozen trees | Deep North structural wood, ships |
| **Void Ore** | Boss drops + deep mining | Endgame weapons and armor |

> 💡 **Pro tip:** clear every ice cave you find. Glacial Crystals and Arctic Moss are the two resources you'll run out of first, and caves are the only reliable source of both.

## Cold Survival Rework — The New Warmth System

The Deep North update completely overhauls how cold works in Valheim. The old "wear Wolf Fur or die" binary is gone — cold is now a **scaled stat**.

### Frost Resistance

Frost resistance is now a value from **0 to 100**. Wearing frost-resistant gear stacks up a resistance score, and different conditions demand different thresholds:

| Condition | Frost Resistance Needed |
|:----------|:------------------------|
| Deep North ambient cold | **60+** to survive without damage |
| Blizzard conditions | **80+** |
| Cold water / swimming | Needs the most — stay out of the water |

### Core Temperature

A new **Core Temperature** bar shows your internal warmth. Campfires, hearths, and hot springs raise it; cold exposure, water, and blizzards lower it. When it hits **zero, you take damage over time** — the same "freeze to death" pressure as the old system, but now with warning.

### Warming Items & Food

| Item | What It Does | Recipe |
|:-----|:-------------|:-------|
| **Hot Stone** | Craftable hand-warmer — carried for passive warmth | Craftable early |
| **Hot Mead** | Temporary temperature boost | Mead base + Arctic Moss |
| **Fire Arrows** | Bonus damage against frost enemies | Arrows + fire |
| **Heartystew** | 30-minute warmth buff | 2 Arctic Moss + 1 Mushroom + 1 Honey |

**Food rebalance:** cooked meats provide more warmth than raw. Heartystew is the single best warming food in the biome — a 30-minute buff that lets you push deep without huddling by a fire every two minutes.

> 🔥 **Blizzard protocol:** when a blizzard hits, if you're more than 60 seconds from shelter, you are in trouble. Drop a campfire behind a wall you build — blizzards only extinguish unprotected fires — or portal out and wait it out at the forward base.

## Frost Forge & New Gear

The **Frost Forge** is a new crafting station, unlocked with the **Glacial Heartstone** dropped by the Frost King. It's the highest-tier workbench in the game and the source of all endgame frost gear.

### Weapons

| Weapon | Type | Effect | Recipe |
|:-------|:-----|:-------|:-------|
| **Glacial Blade** | Greatsword | Frost damage, slows enemies on hit | 20 Void Ore, 10 Glacial Crystals, 5 Linen Thread |
| **Permafrost Bow** | Bow | Arrows freeze the ground beneath the target — creates a slippery surface | 15 Void Ore, 10 Fine Wood, 5 Glacial Crystals |
| **Frost Shatter** | Two-handed hammer | Ground-pound special creates a frost nova | 25 Void Ore, 10 Iron, 10 Glacial Crystals |

### Armor

| Item | Type | Effect | Recipe |
|:-----|:-----|:-------|:-------|
| **Hearthguard Set** | Heavy (chest, legs, helm, cape) | Cold resistance on every piece; set bonus −20% stamina drain in combat | Void Ore + Permafrost Stone + Arctic Moss |
| **Arctic Light Cloak** | Light armor chest | +15% elemental damage — best-in-slot for mages | 10 Void Ore, 10 Linen Thread, 5 Glacial Crystals |

![Valheim Deep North gear progression infographic — from Ashlands prep gear to Void Ore endgame](/assets/images/valheim/deep-north-gear-progression.webp)

> ⚔️ **What to craft first:** Hearthguard chest + legs for the cold resistance, then the Glacial Blade. The frost-slow on the Blade is huge — most Deep North enemies are fast, and a slowed target is a parried target.

## Frost King — Complete Boss Guide

The Frost King is a colossal frozen jotun imprisoned within the **Glacial Heart** — a massive crystal formation at the center of the biome. He's the hardest fight Iron Gate has designed, and you need to treat him like one.

### Summoning the Frost King

| Requirement | Amount |
|:------------|:-------|
| Frost Cores | 10 |
| Glacial Crystals | 5 |
| Players standing on pressure plates | **3 simultaneously** |

Place the Cores and Crystals at the **Altar of Frost** (a runestone at the Glacial Heart), then get **three players onto the pressure plates at the same time**. This is a co-op fight by design — soloing it means juggling plates yourself.

### The Three Phases

**Phase 1 (100% → 60% HP): The Brawler**

- Massive ice fists in sweeping arcs, telegraphed by a **1-second ground crack** visual.
- A **stomp shockwave** that freezes anyone who doesn't dodge-roll *through* it.

**Phase 2 (60% → 25% HP): The Summoner**

- **Ice Spires** erupt from the ground — damaging on rise, but they stay up and work as **cover**.
- **Freezing Breath** in a 120-degree cone — don't stand in front of him.

**Phase 3 (25% → 0% HP): The Berserker**

- He **shatters his own armor** — faster, less defensive.
- Attacks in **3-hit combos**.
- Summons **Frost Wisps** — small adds that explode on proximity, dealing frost damage and applying a **stacking slow**.

![Frost King boss fight infographic — all three phases with mechanics and tips](/assets/images/valheim/frost-king-phases.webp)

### Fight Strategy

1. **Phase 1:** Keep the tank on him, everyone else flank. When you see the ground crack, **don't stand in the arc**. The stomp shockwave is the only thing that should ever land on you.
2. **Phase 2:** Use the Ice Spires as cover from the Freezing Breath. When he starts the breath, break line of sight behind a spire.
3. **Phase 3:** Everything gets faster. One person *must* be dedicated to killing Frost Wisps at range before they reach the group — a stacking slow in Phase 3 is how the fight kills you. Stamina management wins Phase 3: the Hearthguard set's −20% stamina drain is a lifesaver here.

**Recommended loadout:** Hearthguard set (full), Glacial Blade for the frost slow, Frost Resist Mead + Hot Mead stacked, Heartystew for the warmth buff, and a backup portal stone to your forward base.

### Rewards

| Drop | Use |
|:-----|:----|
| **Frost King Trophy** | Mount it on a stone pedestal |
| **Glacial Heartstone** | Unlocks the Frost Forge |
| **Void Ore** (10–15) | Endgame weapons and armor |
| **Frost King's Mantle** | Cape with **full frost immunity** + damage reduction in cold biomes |

> 🏆 The Frost King's Mantle is the best cape in the game, full stop. Full frost immunity means blizzards stop being an emergency — you can walk through them.

## Deep North Progression Path

Follow this order and the biome stays manageable:

1. **Scout the coast** in a Snekkja. Mark ice cave entrances and a good forward-base site.
2. **Build the forward base.** Portal, bed, hearth, and a Frost Forge-ready shelter.
3. **Mine Permafrost Stone and hunt Frost Golems** for Frost Cores — your basic frost-resist gear comes from these.
4. **Clear ice caves** for Glacial Crystals and Arctic Moss.
5. **Craft mid-tier gear** and upgrade what you can at the Frost Forge.
6. **Summon and defeat the Frost King.**
7. **Craft Void Ore gear** — the true endgame set — and finish the final tier.

## FAQ

**Is the Deep North the last biome?**
Yes — it's the eighth and final biome in Valheim's current progression. There's nowhere further north.

**Do I need to kill Fader (Ashlands boss) first?**
You don't *have* to, but you'll want Ashlands-tier gear. The Deep North is tuned for players who've cleared the Ashlands.

**Can I build a permanent base in the Deep North?**
Yes, but only with the new materials. Blizzards destroy unprotected fires and wood structures struggle with the cold — use Permafrost Stone for the frame and keep a hearth burning in an enclosed room.

**What should I do if I keep freezing?**
Stack frost resistance gear to 60+ (80+ for blizzards), keep a Hot Stone in your inventory, and carry Heartystew for the 30-minute warmth buff. The old "Wolf Fur cape is enough" days are over.

**Where do I get Void Ore?**
From the Frost King (10–15 per kill) and from deep mining in the Deep North. It's the rarest resource in the game — every endgame craft in this biome eats it.
