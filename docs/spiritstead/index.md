# Spiritstead 🏡

**Build a cozy village where 35 hidden spirits quietly do the work.** A hand-drawn, Miyazaki-inspired city-builder — restore the Grand Spirit Sanctuary across three biomes, or just build forever in Creative Mode.

## What is Spiritstead?

Spiritstead is a **cozy city-builder** from four-person indie studio **Turbo Dog Games**, set in a hand-illustrated world inspired by Hayao Miyazaki's films. Humans and spirits once lived in harmony; when that harmony broke, the spirits faded away. Your job is to rebuild the village, discover **35 hidden spirits** through strange, playful world interactions, and restore the **Grand Spirit Sanctuary** across three biomes. It looks like a gentle village game, but collect enough spirits and it quietly becomes a satisfying **automation game** where your town runs itself. It released on **Steam on August 6, 2026** at **$9.99** to a Very Positive rating.

## Game Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--------|:------------|
| 🏡 **Cozy City-Building** | Assign villagers jobs, build homes, farms, cafés, markets, taverns and more |
| 🧚 **35 Hidden Spirits** | Summon quirky spirits via strange world rituals — hold a chicken over a bonfire, fling a sheep into the sky |
| ⚙️ **Automation Payoff** | Assign the right spirits and your town runs itself — "a cozy builder that becomes an automation game" |
| ❤️ **Villager Happiness** | Meet needs and build boosters (bonfires, benches, flowers, sauna) to keep the bar healthy |
| 🗺️ **Three Biomes** | Expand through the Green Forest, Snow and Swamp via spirit portals to restore the Grand Spirit Sanctuary |
| 🎣 **Mini-Games** | Bucket fishing, frog jumping and a matching game — each hides a spirit |
| 🎨 **Two Modes** | A ~3–8 hour Adventure mode and a pressure-free Creative sandbox |
| ✍️ **Hand-Drawn Art** | A "living coloring book" style inspired by Hayao Miyazaki films |
