# Necesse 🌍

**Terraria's mining meets RimWorld's colony automation** — a procedurally generated sandbox where you fight, build, and automate a living settlement.

## What is Necesse?

Necesse is an action-RPG + dungeon crawler + colony simulator + survival sandbox rolled into one, developed and published by **Fair Games ApS** (Denmark). You spawn on a procedurally generated island, survive hostile nights, then turn your camp into a fully automated settlement where specialized villagers farm, craft, and fight for you.
