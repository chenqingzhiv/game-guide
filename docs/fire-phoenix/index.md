## What is Three Kingdoms: Fire Phoenix?

Tactical strategy RPG from **Bilibili Games**, based on Chan Mou's manhua that has run for 20+ years across 80+ volumes. Unlike typical Three Kingdoms games, Fire Phoenix centers on the **Strategist Card System** — you build a hand of tactics before battle and execute them on a dynamic grid where terrain shifts mid-fight.

### Key features

- **Strategist Card System**: Build a pre-battle loadout of advisor tactics, play them as cards during combat
- **Dynamic terrain**: Battlefields change mid-battle — flood, fire, fog, collapse, and blessed ground all shift the fight
- **Dual protagonists**: Follow Sima Yi (the calculating strategist) and Liao Yuan Huo (the enigmatic assassin)
- **F2P-friendly**: All strategist cards earnable through gameplay, not just the gacha
- **Cross-platform**: Play on Android and PC with shared save data

## Getting Started

Jump straight into the main story and push until Chapter 5 unlocks Strategist Trials. Don't touch the gacha early. Focus on building a balanced core team of 4 with one tank, one healer, one DPS, and one flex. Save your diamonds for Limited Strategist Card banners — they scale better than character pulls.
